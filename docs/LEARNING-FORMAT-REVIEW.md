# Learning format without fixed learner labels · 2026-10-17

The existing protocol already adapted to demonstrated work, but did not explicitly distinguish presentation preferences, access needs and fixed learning-style claims. The new short section addresses that gap; it is not a learner classifier or a new diagnostic form.

## Sources actually inspected

- Pashler et al., 2008 issue: [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1539-6053.2009.01038.x), [accessible paper PDF](https://www.brucehayes.org/Teaching/papers/PashlerEtAl2008OnLearningStyles.pdf). Inspected evidence criteria, figure explanation and clarification sections. Their matching claim requires different methods to benefit different classified groups under appropriate comparison; task differences and individual responses are not denied. Publisher HTML failed in this pass; the paper PDF was available.
- Clinton-Lisell & Litzinger, 2024: [published meta-analysis](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2024.1428732/full). Inspected inclusion criteria, quality coding, results, sensitivity analyses and limitations. Across 21 studies/1,712 participants the reported aggregate g was 0.31, but only about 26% of outcome measures showed the relevant crossover pattern. Studies were single-session, with reporting/quality limits and unexplained variation. The authors did not recommend widespread matching. This prevents claiming that every study or aggregate is exactly null; it does not supply an individual prescription.
- [Prospective-teacher experiment, published online 2024, 2025 volume](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11251-024-09689-1): inspected design, sample, procedure and moderation results. The 222-person study found no significant style-by-presentation interaction for learning performance. This concerns its particular participants, materials and measures, not every learner or accessible format.
- Hattie & O’Leary, 2025: [reflection on meta-analyses](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10648-025-10002-w). Inspected abstract and conceptual discussion distinguishing matching from correlates/strategies. This is a field reflection, not a new randomized trial. Its headline estimate was not pooled with the 2024 estimate or used to settle all methodological differences.

## Adopted behavior and limits

Respect requested formats and accessibility; choose representations for the task and evidence. Do not infer a lifelong type from liking diagrams, force a style inventory, or deny an accessible alternative. Compare actual relevant performance without pretending a preference is proof of efficacy. These are design decisions informed by the distinction, not trials of this Skill.

Four new authored scenarios cover a fixed-style inference, text access for a lesson, direct reformatting, and interpretation of the nonzero meta-analytic result. Schema validation is not model behavior or learner-outcome validation. No diagnostic labels, personal records, classroom data, paid experiments or new dependencies were created.
