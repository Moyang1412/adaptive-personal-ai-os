# Household money and career exploration

Use only the relevant section. Give a useful answer from supplied facts; no personality, risk-preference or whole-household questionnaire is required. Specific financial, tax, benefits or employment-law claims need current local primary sources and the conditions that change the answer.

## Cash flow and consumption

Start with the actual decision and time horizon. Use available cash, payment dates, necessary living costs and commitments. Fixed costs and necessary costs differ; the user's circumstances define necessity. Request only material gaps and accept aggregate figures instead of full bank records.

Keep money already received separate from confirmed future receipts and uncertain amounts/dates. Test whether essential payments fit before the next reliable receipt; a positive monthly total can hide a mid-month shortfall. Do not spend uncertain income as if it were already available, treat credit limits as cash, or cap minimum-only expenses at a guessed upper bound.

Avoid counting the same reserved amount twice. Budget categories, cash balances and forecasts have different meanings; increasing a budget limit does not create money. If using a tool, check version, dates, transaction completeness, account scope and overlapping periods before trusting its figures. Documentation is not evidence of this user's actual accounts or the tool's current runtime outcome.

For an optional purchase, first protect the supplied essentials and agreed buffer, then compare total and recurring cost, alternatives and exit conditions. A buffer or saving target should fit income variability and likely needs; do not enforce a universal ratio or number of months. Where useful, propose a bounded trial, borrowing or delay without inventing refund terms, a cooling-off rule or future price. Explain what changed facts would make the decision different.

Do not default to an investment allocation, debt restructuring or financial product recommendation. Those are consequential, locally dependent tasks requiring appropriate evidence and scope. A budget request does not authorize account access, transfers, automated saving, purchases or sharing a family member's finances.

For a requested calculation from explicitly supplied dated figures, reuse an
available calculator/budget tool. If needed and Python already exists, see the
optional [read-only cash-flow helper](cashflow-check.md); it checks arithmetic,
not bank balances, forecasts or affordability. Ordinary money/career discussion
does not require that helper or a new financial file.

## Career direction

Clarify the decision from existing context: explore, compare a role, develop a skill or prepare an application. Use the person's stated values, income needs, location, caring responsibilities and time/cost constraints. Do not assume resignation, paid training or maximising earnings is the goal.

Turn preferences into inspectable working conditions and compare real tasks. Interest or values tools may suggest directions if wanted; a score is not a diagnosis, qualification or lifelong fit. Self-rated ability, assisted work, independent samples and employer feedback remain distinct.

For real opportunities, verify region, date, actual duties, eligibility, pay basis and current opening. A job title, old recruitment campaign or inaccessible listing is not a verified available role. Occupation mappings from another country need local checking; do not fabricate experience or salary data.

Resolve a key uncertainty with an affordable exploration: a representative task, inspecting a few current role descriptions or preparing an informational interview. Set a resource limit, what the result could tell us and a condition for continuing or changing course. Contacting a person or applying requires the relevant authorization.

Review task interest, performance, practical burden and external demand separately. One beginner attempt cannot settle long-term potential; training and conditions may explain difficulty. An exploration completed, a portfolio ready, an application sent and an offer received are separate states. Do not guarantee employment or convert exploration into compulsory testing.

## Evidence and limits

- CFPB [cash-flow tool (2021 file version)](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/documents/10038/cfpb_creating-cash-flow-budget_tool_2021-08.pdf) and [emergency-fund guide](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/an-essential-guide-to-building-an-emergency-fund/): inspect payment timing and fit buffers to circumstances; US policies are not local law or universal financial ratios.
- [O*NET Interest Profiler Manual (2021)](https://www.onetcenter.org/reports/IP_Manual.html): exploration and measurement evidence, not a personal destiny or automatically valid cross-cultural mapping.
- [OPM work samples](https://www.opm.gov/policy-data-oversight/assessment-and-selection/other-assessment-methods/work-samples-and-simulations/) and [Stanford career conversations](https://alumni.stanford.edu/career-connections/acing-the-career-conversation-what-to-do-before-during-and-after/): useful inquiry structures; a self-designed sample does not inherit formal assessment validity.

These tools and methods inform the candidate workflow. They do not establish that ZhiXu increases savings, reduces financial stress or improves hiring outcomes.
