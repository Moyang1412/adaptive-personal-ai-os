# Approval Policy

## Always ask before

- Publishing, deploying, merging, pushing to an external repository, or sending externally.
- Paying, purchasing, deleting, or taking an irreversible action.
- Editing credentials, security settings, production data, or another person's private data.

## Can proceed when already authorized

- Read-only inspection.
- Reversible local edits.
- Tests, linting, builds, and reports within the declared workspace.
