# Roles

| Role | Responsibility | Tool or person | Approval boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| Owner | Direction, values, final quality judgment | User | Keeps final authority |
| Planner | Goals, constraints, priorities, next actions | AI workflow | No external side effects |
| Executor | Files, code, tests, local changes | Codex or another approved tool | Works within declared scope |
| Verifier | Independent checks and regression review | Independent verifier or approved review tool | Reports findings; does not silently publish |
